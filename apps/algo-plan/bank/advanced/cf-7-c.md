## 题面

**Codeforces Beta Round 7**　7-C　（官方难度 rating 1800）

原题名：Line

**题意（中文）**

直线方程 Ax+By+C=0。求一个**整数坐标**的点（坐标绝对值不超过 5·10^18）落在该直线上；不存在这样的点输出 -1。

**原题面（英文）**

A line on the plane is described by an equation Ax + By + C = 0. You are to find any point on this line, whose coordinates are integer numbers from  - 5·1018 to 5·1018 inclusive, or to find out that such points do not exist.

**输入**

The first line contains three integers A, B and C ( - 2·109 ≤ A, B, C ≤ 2·109) — corresponding coefficients of the line equation. It is guaranteed that A2 + B2 > 0.

**输出**

If the required point exists, output its coordinates, otherwise output -1.

**限制**：每组测试 1 second，256 megabytes

## 测试用例

|---|---|
| 2 5 3 | 6 -3 |
